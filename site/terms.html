Last reviewed: 21 October 2026.

Project status and scope

The seed contains historical GSM8K source traces. It has not been human step-annotated, and this site reports no human agreement score or first-error finding. Upstream final-answer metadata is not a substitute for human step labels. Read the repository’s datasheet and pre-annotation pilot report before reusing the material.

Site examples are previews of the task structure, not annotation decisions. The preview controls do not submit, save, or transmit a judgment.
